Insofar as we obtain the consent of the data subject for processing operations involving personal data, Art. 6 (1) (a) of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) serves as the legal basis. Where the processing of personal data is necessary for the performance of a contract to which the data subject is party, Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR serves as the legal basis; this also applies to processing operations necessary for carrying out pre-contractual measures. Where processing is necessary to fulfil a legal obligation to which our company is subject, Art. 6 (1) (c) GDPR serves as the legal basis.

  • 存在要求更正或删除、限制处理或反对处理的权利；
  • 存在向监管机构投诉的权利；
  • 当个人数据并非从数据主体处收集时，关于数据来源的所有可用信息。

（2）更正权

若与您相关且被处理的个人数据不正确或不完整，您有权要求控制者予以更正和/或补充。控制者应立即进行更正。

（3）限制处理权

在以下前提下，您可要求限制对相关个人数据的处理：

（4）删除权

在下列理由之一成立时，您可要求立即删除相关个人数据，我们亦有义务立即删除：数据对于其收集目的已不再必要；您撤回了处理所依据的同意且无其他法律依据；您依 GDPR 第 21 条提出反对且不存在优先的正当理由；数据被不合法处理；或为履行法律义务而必须删除。

（5）被通知权

若您已主张更正、删除或限制处理，控制者有义务将其通知所有曾被披露相关个人数据的接收方，除非此举不可能或需付出不成比例的努力。您有权要求获知这些接收方。

（6）数据可携权

您有权以结构化、通用且机器可读的格式获取您向控制者提供的相关个人数据，并在技术可行且不损害他人权利的前提下，将其传输给另一控制者（前提为处理基于同意或合同、且以自动化方式进行）。

（7）反对权

基于您特定情形产生的理由，您有权随时反对对基于 GDPR 第 6 条第 1 款 e 项或 f 项进行的、与您相关的个人数据处理。

If your personal data is processed, you are a data subject within the meaning of the GDPR and you have the following rights vis-à-vis the controller:

(1) Right of access

You can request confirmation from the controller as to whether personal data concerning you is being processed. If so, you can request information about the following:

  • the purposes for which the personal data is processed;
  • the categories of personal data being processed;
  • the recipients or categories of recipients to whom the data has been or will be disclosed;
  • the planned storage period or, if not possible, the criteria for determining it;
  • the existence of a right to correction, deletion, restriction of processing or objection;
  • the existence of a right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority;
  • all available information about the origin of the data if not collected from the data subject.

(2) Right to correction

You have a right to correction and/or completion if the processed personal data concerning you is incorrect or incomplete. The controller must carry out the correction without delay.

(3) Right to restriction of processing

Under the following conditions you can request the restriction of processing:

  • if you contest the accuracy of the data for a period enabling the controller to verify it;
  • the processing is unlawful and you refuse deletion, instead requesting restriction of use;
  • the controller no longer needs the data, but you need it to assert, exercise or defend legal claims;
  • if you have objected pursuant to Art. 21 (1) GDPR and it is not yet determined whether the controller's grounds override yours.

(4) Right to deletion

You can request that personal data be deleted without delay, and we are obliged to do so, if one of the following applies: the data is no longer necessary for its purposes; you revoke your consent and there is no other legal basis; you object pursuant to Art. 21 GDPR and there are no overriding grounds; the data was processed unlawfully; or deletion is required to fulfil a legal obligation.

(5) Right to be informed

If you have asserted the right to correction, deletion or restriction, the controller must notify all recipients to whom the data was disclosed, unless this proves impossible or involves disproportionate effort. You have the right to be informed about these recipients.

(6) Right to data portability

You have the right to receive the personal data you provided in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format, and to transmit it to another controller — where technically feasible and without adversely affecting others' rights — provided the processing is based on consent or a contract and carried out by automated means.

(7) Right to object

You have the right, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to object at any time to the processing of personal data concerning you carried out on the basis of Art. 6 (1) (e) or (f) GDPR.
